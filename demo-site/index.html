Proof of Solvency

Each epoch we publish a zero-knowledge proof that our customer liabilities were computed honestly from real balances, and that our reserves cover them. Everything on this page is read from the blockchain — no wallet, and nothing you have to take on trust from us.

AssetPriceOracle round

The contract pinned these oracle rounds, valued the reserves at them, and verified the proof against them. A proof built on any other table is rejected. Anyone can refetch these rounds and recompute the totals independently.

Every customer balance is committed to inside this single value. Individual balances are never published — check your own account to confirm yours is included in it.
