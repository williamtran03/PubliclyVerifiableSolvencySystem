Proof of Solvency

Each epoch we publish a zero-knowledge proof that, for every asset separately, what we owe customers is covered by reserves we have proven we control. Everything on this page is read from the blockchain — no wallet, and nothing you have to take on trust from us.

The proof shows customer liabilities in each asset are at most its floor; the contract checked the approved reserves hold at least the floor. No price is involved, so a surplus in one asset cannot hide a shortfall in another. Prices only value the reserves in USD, at pinned oracle rounds anyone can refetch.

Liability commitment

Every customer balance is committed to inside this single value, bound to this epoch of this registry. Neither individual balances nor the totals are published — check your own account to confirm yours is included in it.
