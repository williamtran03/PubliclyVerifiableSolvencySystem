Verify your balance is included

Pick your account, confirm the balances you believe you hold, and this page recomputes the commitment from your own inclusion proof. Nothing here is taken on trust — if the numbers you enter are not what the exchange committed to, the check fails.

Commitment, totals and prices are public on-chain. Individual customer balances are never published — the proof establishes the total without revealing who holds what.
