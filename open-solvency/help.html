For customers · No wallet needed

Verify your balances

  1. Choose a proof method. Open the application, keep For customers selected and choose Zero-Knowledge Circuit, Merkle-Sum Tree or KZG without a Circuit. Your customer proof must belong to that method.
  2. Load the demo snapshot. Click Use Sepolia deployment. This fills in the connection and loads the latest snapshot. For another registry, enter its RPC endpoint and Registry address, then click Load latest snapshot.
  3. Review the snapshot. Check the epoch, publication time and freshness message, then review the reserves and liabilities shown for each asset. Some methods keep liabilities private. The Etherscan link opens the registry on the block explorer.
  4. Prepare your customer proof. For the demo, use the Public fictional customer details and click Download fictional customer proof. In Verify my balances, enter the displayed customer ID, select that JSON file and enter the displayed account secret. If no matching example is available, use a proof for the loaded epoch.
  5. Enter the balances. For the fictional example, choose Proof units (integers) under Amount units and copy each displayed asset amount exactly. For your own proof, use your own records and your customer-chosen secret. Token amounts accepts normal token amounts where the asset metadata is available.
  6. Run the check. Click Verify proof and read the result. Try changing a balance to see the check fail. After changing the method or registry, load its snapshot and select a matching proof again.
For companies

Review and publish a snapshot

Switch to For companies, choose the method and load your registry. Follow the method-specific preparation steps shown in the application.

  1. To check an existing publication, select its artifact under Compare an already published artifact and click Compare with registry.
  2. To publish, generate the next epoch's artifacts with the project tools. Under Submit the next epoch, select the files requested for your method and enter oracle round IDs if shown.
  3. Review the files and registry, then click Publish with company wallet. Use the authorized company wallet on the correct network and review the wallet transaction before confirming. Deliver customer proof bundles privately.
